# CV bullet

- Led a 4-person course project (CIE6004) comparing Point-E text-to-3D generative augmentation against geometric augmentation for PointNet on ModelNet40. I designed the 6-configuration ablation and built the Point-E prompt/generation/filtering pipeline and the geometric augmentation. With 25 training samples per class (1 seed, N = 910 test), geometric augmentation reached 73.1% test accuracy vs 65.1% with Point-E, against a 66.6% baseline.

Source: `results/NUMBERS.md` (Exp1 / Exp4 / Exp5).
